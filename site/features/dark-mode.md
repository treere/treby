
# Dark Mode

Treby ships with a full dark theme. A toggle in the top navigation (next to the language selector) lets you switch between **Light**, **Dark**, and **System** modes.

![Dark Mode Dashboard](/screenshots/25-dark-mode.png)

## How it works

- **System** (default): Treby follows your operating system's light/dark preference automatically
- **Light**: always uses the light theme
- **Dark**: always uses the dark theme

Your explicit choice is saved in your browser's `localStorage`, so it persists across visits. When set to **System**, the theme updates live if your OS switches between light and dark.

## Where to find the toggle

The theme toggle sits in the top navigation bar, right next to the language switcher. It is available on every page once you're signed in — and the choice applies app-wide, including the login page.

## Everything is themed

No surface is left out — dashboards, the pipeline board, candidate profiles, analytics, settings, public career pages, and the auth screens all respect the active theme.
