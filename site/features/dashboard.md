# Dashboard

Land on an at-a-glance view of what needs your attention the moment you log in. The entire dashboard is bilingual — switch between Italian and English in **Settings → Language** and every heading, label, empty state and notification updates instantly.

![Dashboard](/screenshots/04-dashboard.png)

## My Actions

The dashboard surfaces the work that belongs to *you* so you don't have to scan the whole pipeline board to figure out what's expected of you.

- **Scorecards to fill** — interviews where you're the examiner but your scorecard isn't submitted yet. Each row shows the candidate, job, and interview time with a direct **Fill scorecard** button that opens the same scorecard form used across the app.
- **Waiting on others** — applications at an interview stage blocked by other people's outstanding work (another examiner's missing scorecard or an interview not yet completed). This section is read-only, so you can see *why* something is stuck and follow up.

When you have nothing pending, the panel shows an "All caught up" empty state.

## What else is here

- **Upcoming Interviews** — interviews you're scheduled for in the next 7 days
- **Stale Candidates** — candidates with no recent activity, so you can follow up
- **Pipeline Overview** — candidate counts per stage for each open job
- **Recent Activity** — a live log of what's been happening across the tenant
- **Weekly Stats** — applications, interviews, offers, and hires this week
