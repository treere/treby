# Interview Scheduling

Let candidates self-schedule interviews, integrated with Google Calendar.

## How it works

1. A team member sends a scheduling link to a candidate
2. The candidate picks an available time slot
3. A Google Calendar event is created with a Google Meet link
4. Both parties receive email confirmations

## Features

- **Self-scheduling**: candidates choose from available slots, no back-and-forth
- **Calendar sync**: events appear on the team member's Google Calendar
- **Google Meet**: automatic video conferencing links
- **Availability rules**: configure when you're available for interviews
- **Timezone-aware**: automatic timezone detection for candidates

## Configuration

Connect your Google Calendar in Settings to enable interview scheduling. The integration uses OAuth 2.0 with encrypted token storage.
